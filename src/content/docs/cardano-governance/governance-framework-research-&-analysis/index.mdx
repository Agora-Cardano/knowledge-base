Cardano's governance framework continues to evolve through research, community assessment, and technical proposals. This section examines documented governance challenges, the evidence used to assess them, and initiatives developed to explore potential improvements.

The articles distinguish research findings and proposals from changes that have been formally adopted or implemented.
