# Agora Knowledge Base

**Cardano Governance · Education · Knowledge**

The **Agora Knowledge Base** is the educational and knowledge-sharing branch of Agora, an independent initiative focused on improving the quality and decentralization of decision-making in the Cardano ecosystem.

It is designed to organize, explain, and make accessible **consolidated knowledge** about **Cardano governance and its institutional environment**.

The Knowledge Base focuses on established and well-supported knowledge that can help readers understand Cardano's governance, institutions, and the concepts that underpin them. It combines original content produced by Agora with selected material from other ecosystem organizations, community initiatives, and external sources.

## Purpose

The Agora Knowledge Base aims to:

- make important Cardano governance and institutional knowledge easier to find, navigate, and understand;
- consolidate information that is fragmented across different sources;
- connect readers with existing high-quality resources and, where useful, organize, summarize, or localize that material so it can be more easily understood and accessed;
- provide deeper educational material on subjects that benefit from structured analysis and synthesis;
- improve access to relevant resources in multiple languages;
- provide a neutral knowledge resource that can support more informed participation in the Cardano ecosystem.

The Knowledge Base is intended to help readers understand not only **how Cardano governance works**, but also the concepts and properties necessary to critically understand it.

## Editorial Approach

The Knowledge Base follows a few basic principles:

- **Consolidated knowledge should be clearly distinguished from opinion or advocacy.**
- **Content should explain concepts and systems rather than promote particular positions.**
- **Claims should be supported by evidence and relevant references.**
- **Primary and authoritative sources should be used whenever available.**
- **Where concepts or interpretations remain contested, relevant perspectives should be presented fairly.**
- **Original synthesis should be used when useful information is fragmented across multiple sources.**

There are two main types of content in the Knowledge Base:

1. **Original Agora content** — educational material researched, written, and produced by Agora. This content is licensed under the repository's **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** license, unless otherwise stated.
2. **Curated and localized content** — relevant material produced by third parties that Agora makes easier to access through summaries, localization, structured references, embeds, or direct links, with appropriate attribution and licensing.

Third-party content remains subject to its original author's or publisher's licensing terms. The Agora repository license does not automatically apply to external material that is linked, embedded, reproduced, translated, or otherwise incorporated into the Knowledge Base. Where third-party material is reproduced or adapted, the applicable licensing and attribution requirements will be respected.

The Knowledge Base does not aim to prescribe governance decisions or promote particular political positions within Cardano.

## Initial Scope

The first version of the Knowledge Base is organized around two primary domains:

| Domain | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Cardano Governance** | Governance actors, mechanisms, processes, concepts, and analytical dimensions. |
| **Institutions & Organizations** | Organizations, committees, working groups, and institutional structures relevant to Cardano. |

### Cardano Governance

This section contains consolidated knowledge concerning Cardano's governance system, including its actors, mechanisms, processes, concepts, and analytical dimensions.

Initial original material will include subjects such as:

- Governance Accountability
- Governance Sustainability
- Governance Decentralization
- Governance Participation

Foundational topics that are already well documented elsewhere may instead be summarized, localized, embedded, or linked to appropriate existing resources. The purpose is not to recreate material unnecessarily, but to make useful existing knowledge easier to discover and access, while adding original material where there is a meaningful gap.

### Institutions & Organizations

This section provides information concerning organizations and institutional structures relevant to the Cardano ecosystem.

It may include organizations, committees, working groups, and other structures whose roles are useful for understanding how the ecosystem operates.

This section will be expanded progressively as relevant material becomes available.

## Languages

The initial language scope of the Knowledge Base is:

- **English**
- **Portuguese (Brazil)**

The Knowledge Base may expand to additional languages as demand and available resources justify it. The increasing availability of AI-assisted translation can reduce the effort required for broad multilingual coverage, while human review and targeted localization may still be useful where cultural, linguistic, or contextual adaptation is important.

Where practical, equivalent content should be maintained within the same Knowledge Base rather than as independent language-specific projects.

## Sources and References

The Knowledge Base is intended to draw from authoritative, institutional, academic, and high-quality community sources.

The detailed source and evidence policy is still being developed. In practice, this will involve an active process of **curation and periodic updating**, with attention to source quality, relevance, attribution, licensing, and whether information remains current and well supported.

A primary starting point is:

- [Cardano Docs](https://docs.cardano.org/)

Cardano Docs functions not only as documentation but also as a gateway to other parts of the Cardano information ecosystem, including developer resources, governance documentation, educational initiatives, technical resources, research references, ecosystem tools, and community resources.

Relevant resources include, among others:

- [Cardano Developer Portal](https://developers.cardano.org/)
- [Essential Cardano](https://www.essentialcardano.io/)
- [Intersect](https://intersectmbo.org/)
- [Cardano Academy](https://academy.cardanofoundation.org/)
- [Cardano Forum](https://forum.cardano.org/)
- [Cardano Cube](https://cardanocube.io/)
- [Built on Cardano](https://built-on-cardano.com/)
- [Cardano Improvement Proposals (CIPs)](https://cips.cardano.org/)
- Research papers, technical specifications, and other relevant primary documentation.

These resources serve different purposes and will not necessarily be treated in the same way. The Knowledge Base may use them as primary references, complementary sources, directories, or gateways to more specialized information depending on the subject.

The inclusion of a source does not imply that all of its content is automatically treated as authoritative. Source selection, attribution, licensing, freshness, and the distinction between primary documentation and community-generated material will form part of the Knowledge Base's ongoing editorial process.

## Relationship with Other Agora Initiatives

The Knowledge Base has a distinct role within Agora.

**Agora Knowledge Base** focuses on consolidated educational and knowledge resources.

**Agora Research Bureau** focuses on original research and analysis of specific questions, data, events, and governance actions.

**Rashomon** focuses on documented historical evidence, reputation, and track records.

Research or evidence produced through other Agora initiatives may be referenced by the Knowledge Base when relevant, but their functions remain distinct.

## Scope Expansion

The Knowledge Base is intentionally focused on **Cardano governance and institutions**, rather than attempting to become a general-purpose repository covering every aspect of the Cardano ecosystem.

New top-level domains should therefore be introduced only when there is a clear connection to this scope and sufficient content to justify treating a subject as an independent knowledge domain.

The Knowledge Base may link to or embed external resources covering other areas of Cardano when those resources are useful for understanding governance or institutions, but it does not aim to maintain its own comprehensive directory or database of all Cardano projects.

## Knowledge Infrastructure

The Knowledge Base is maintained in a [dedicated GitHub repository](https://github.com/Agora-Cardano/knowledge-base) within the Agora initiative, where its content can be version-controlled, reviewed, and updated transparently.

The public presentation and navigation layer may evolve as the project develops.
